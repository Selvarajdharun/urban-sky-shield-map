URBAN SKY SHIELD — METHODOLOGY
Objective
AI-assisted spatial planning for understanding light-pollution pressure and identifying potential dark-sky protection areas in Tamil Nadu.

Conceptual Inputs
• Satellite night-light proxy / indicator
• Urban and built-up influence
• Road / infrastructure influence
• Population / activity indicators
• Future field-based sky-brightness measurements

Processing Concept
Multi-source spatial indicators → AI-assisted spatial analysis → pollution-pressure visualization → mitigation planning.

Mitigation Scenarios
Shielded street lighting, adaptive dimming, late-night lighting control and dark-sky protected zones can be explored as planning scenarios.

Scientific Scope
The current map is a conceptual prototype. Displayed zones are planning visualizations, not direct ground measurements.

No fabricated AI accuracy, R², Bortle measurements or field-validation results are represented.
