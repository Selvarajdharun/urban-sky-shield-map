Explainable Factors

Conceptually, urban density, road/infrastructure intensity, night-light indicators and other available spatial factors can influence predicted pressure. Relative influence values are not quantified here because the model has not yet been trained and validated with a reference dataset.

Validation Roadmap

Future validation can combine satellite night-light datasets, calibrated sky-brightness observations, field measurements, weather information and independent locations for testing.

Data & Science

Why Light Pollution Matters

Excessive and poorly directed artificial light can affect astronomical observation, night-time ecosystems and visibility of the night sky.

Night-Light Proxy

Satellite-derived night-light information can act as a spatial indicator of artificial lighting activity. It should not be treated as a direct measurement of human-visible sky brightness.

Spatial Modelling

Urban density, road infrastructure, built-up indicators and other contextual variables can be combined in a future explainable spatial-prediction workflow.

AI-Assisted Prediction

The proposed AI layer is currently a planning concept. No training accuracy, R² value, field-validation score or predictive performance is claimed by this prototype.

Future Dark-Sky Validation

Candidate areas should be validated through field observations, calibrated sky-quality measurements and independent datasets before being classified as officially protected dark-sky locations.
