URBAN SKY SHIELD
Interactive Tamil Nadu Night-Light & Dark-Sky Research Map

Research Map

Urban Sky Shield visualizes satellite night-light information and a planning framework for dark-sky protection in Tamil Nadu.

NASA VIIRS is a satellite-based indicator of artificial night lighting. It is not a direct Bortle or ground sky-brightness measurement.

The coloured city zones are planning/visualization categories, not invented field measurements.

How to use: switch between light and dark basemaps, turn the NASA night-light layer on/off, search a place, zoom, drag and tap a coloured zone for details.
