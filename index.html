URBAN SKY SHIELD

AI-Assisted Mapping & Prediction Framework for Light-Pollution Planning in Tamil Nadu.

This prototype demonstrates how satellite nighttime-light proxies, urban indicators and spatial analysis can support dark-sky protection.

  • Current pressure zones are conceptual.
  • No artificial AI accuracy is claimed.
  • No measured sky-brightness values are fabricated.
  • Field validation is part of the future roadmap.
