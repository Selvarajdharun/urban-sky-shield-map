Night-light Proxy

Satellite-derived night-light information can be used as an indicator of artificial-light pressure. It is a proxy and should not be interpreted as direct measurement of sky brightness.

Validation Roadmap

Future validation can combine real satellite datasets, calibrated field observations, sky-quality measurements, weather information and local infrastructure data.

Scientific Status

The current pressure visualization is conceptual / planning visualization. No measured experimental accuracy, R², field-derived percentage reduction or trained AI performance is claimed by this interface.

AI-Assisted Spatial Planning

The proposed AI workflow combines spatial indicators to support explainable light-pollution planning.
